      How to order a printed copy

      On the book’s page, write your name, phone, city and address and press the button. Your order opens as a WhatsApp message; send it. No account to create and no online payment.

      Delivery and payment

      We confirm your order on WhatsApp with the book’s price, the delivery cost and when it will arrive. Payment is on delivery (demo, not settled yet).

      Delivery and returns

      Delivery in 3 to 5 days, charge Rs 250. Free on orders of Rs 2,500 or more. If a book arrives damaged or wrong, send us a photo on WhatsApp within seven days of delivery and we will replace it at our cost. Demo

      Quran and hadith references

      Verses of the Quran and hadith in our books are taken only from the publisher’s own checked files, word for word. No reference is written from memory.
